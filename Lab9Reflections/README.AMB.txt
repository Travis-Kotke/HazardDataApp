I used the Copilot terminal that is integrated on VsCode as the AI to develop my page
which was the Quick Stats Screen.  On this page all I had to implement was a
summary news feed, a table displaying recent accidents as well as a graph. 
As this was my first time working with integrated AI on VsCode I did not really know
how to approach developing the page at first. So what I did was provide the screenshot
of my page from our team's developed storyboard.  That was a good basis step to 
begin with as the ai developed a mockup of my page however it didnt fit my formatting entirely.
As I went along with the AI I had to supervise what it was doing and had to consistantly read
through actions it wanted me to approve which I slowly began to just skim as they were
far beyond my understanding of random machine gibberish of error messages. Everytime the AI
finished its message I would check its work by running the app to see if it fit my standards.
Eventually through trial and error I got to a point where I liked the layout of the page but it just
wasn't displaying properly as there was no data to call.  So I had my AI generate me
some dummy data that I could use to display what the actual page would likely look like.
I made sure to tell the AI to keep the dummy data not too integrated as it was only placeholder.
Overall, my page was quite simple as it was mostly just displaying data from a database
that wasn't set up yet.  The hardest thing about the lab was learning how to work with
the integrated AI and tweak my prompts to develop the code in a manner i saw fit.
